[DEPLOYMENT DECISION DEFENSE]
1. Raising the recursion limit via sys.setrecursionlimit() is DANGEROUS in production because:
   - It risks an uncatchable OS-level C-stack overflow segmentation fault.
   - It consumes unnecessary memory for activation records on the heap/stack.
2. Rewriting with an iterative loop is the strictly superior engineering decision:
   - Eliminates call overhead entirely.
   - Reduces space complexity from O(n) to strictly O(1).
   - Safely scales to n = 100,000+ with zero crashes.
